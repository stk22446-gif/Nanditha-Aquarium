Aquarium Accessories & Filtration

Ensure crystal clear water and a healthy environment for your aquatic friends.

  • High Efficiency Filters: Hang-on-back, biochemical sponge filters, power heads & multi-stage canister systems.
  • Quiet Air Pumps & Aeration: Single and dual nozzle silent diaphragms with airstones & check valves.
  • Aquatic Plant & RGB Lights: Full-spectrum LED lamps promoting photosynthesis and vivid fish color pop.
  • Auto Cut-Off Heaters: Shatterproof quartz glass thermostat heaters (50W to 300W) for stable temperature.
  • Live Plants & Hardscape: Anubias, Amazon swords, Java moss, Seiryu stones, Dragon stones & aged driftwood.
  • Maintenance & Water Care: Siphon gravel vacuums, magnetic glass scrapers, anti-chlorine conditioners & bio starters.
🦜

Bird Cages & Enrichment Accessories

Spacious, safe, and stimulating equipment for happy, energetic birds.

  • Safe Enamelled Cages: Spacious non-toxic powder coated wire habitats with slide-out cleaning trays.
  • Natural Wood Perches: Ergonomic rough-bark branch perches to prevent bumblefoot and promote healthy claws.
  • Hygienic Feeders & Drinkers: Twin stainless steel clamping cups, automatic gravity water dispensers & treat holders.
  • Enrichment Toys & Ladders: Colorful wooden chew ladders, rope boings, foraging balls & bell pendants.
  • Breeding & Nesting Boxes: Naturally ventilated wooden nesting chambers customized for budgies and cockatiels.
  • Health & Calming Accessories: Mineral calcium pecking blocks, cuttlefish bone brackets, and breathable night cage covers.
